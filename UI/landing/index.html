The Peer-Vetted Advantage

Why CampusCompass?

Traditional platforms overwhelm you with thousands of elective options, paid certificates, or AI-generated roadmaps that change every time you refresh.

CampusCompass brings structure back to your semesters. We curate a single, peer-vetted roadmap for each tech career path, helping you ignore the noise and focus on what actually moves the needle during college.

Unlike other platforms, CampusCompass does not use AI recommendations. It relies on curated, peer-vetted static JSON roadmaps. This ensures the curriculum is focused, structured, and free of AI hallucinations.

The dashboard reads the skill tags on your profile. It performs a case-insensitive search matching those tag strings against the topic names and descriptions in your roadmap. When matches are found, it checks them off and recalculates your overall progress.
